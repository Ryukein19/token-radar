The pre-token protocol board — which protocol records no token yet, what it could be worth, and how to farm it. Fitted on the snapshot's own figures, never on a hand-kept list of names.

What "records no token" means. A protocol records no token when DefiLlama records neither a CoinGecko id nor a symbol for it. That is the record, not proof it has none — and a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that trades is not a token that does not exist yet, so the board never ranks one.

What the odds are. The odds are the peers' record, not a forecast: among protocols like this one, how often does one record a token? The page never says a token will launch, or when. A "kin of a token" badge means the name stem matches a protocol whose token already trades — a heuristic from the name alone.

What the verdicts are. FARM / MARGINAL / SKIP are costed claims at the ticket and drop share you set: slice = implied × drop share × (ticket / TVL), net = slice − fees − time at $25/h. FARM needs net > $25 and net/hour > $25. Change the ticket or the drop share and every verdict recomputes.
